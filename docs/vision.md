# Vision

The Executive Support System exists to help people who struggle with:

- rapid idea loss — a thought appears and disappears before it can be captured;
- context loss — returning to a task after an interruption means reconstructing what you were doing;
- identifying next actions — knowing what happened does not automatically produce clarity on what to do next;
- continuous monitoring — tracking a market, a research area, or a project requires sustained attention that is difficult to maintain.

These challenges are common among people with executive dysfunction, but they affect knowledge workers broadly.

## What we are building toward

A lightweight, Notion-centered product that:

- captures thoughts with minimal friction;
- preserves enough context to resume interrupted work;
- surfaces likely next actions without requiring the user to reconstruct them;
- stays AI-optional, so it works even without AI assistance.

## The role of the AI agent team

The agent team exists to extend what the human maintainer can pay attention to. Agents gather, structure, and surface information. They do not make decisions or take consequential actions independently.

## What this is not

This is not a general productivity tool, a task manager, or a replacement for existing tools. It is a focused module with a defined scope, operating as the first step toward a broader system called Nexus.
